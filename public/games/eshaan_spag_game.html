❓ How to Play

ControlsWASDARROW KEYSDOUBLE-TAP ←/→ TO DODGE1–4 ANSWERP PAUSE

Steer around traffic and hazards, collect coins, and drive through glowing SPAG power-ups. Double-tap left or right for a fast sideways dodge. Answer correctly to activate the reward.

Milestone bosses: every five levels, a random survival event begins. Avoid collisions for 30 seconds while the boss timer counts down. SPAG questions pause until the event ends.
